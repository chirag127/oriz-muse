What it is

Muse is a free AI writing studio: draft short stories, poems, song lyrics, blog posts, essays, and screenplays. Seed a topic to generate, hand it a draft to continue in your voice, or rewrite any passage in a chosen style and tone.

Key features

  • Generate — pick a genre, seed a topic, tune style, tone, and length; text streams in live.
  • Continue — matches your voice, tense, and style and adds what comes next.
  • Rewrite — recast a passage into Noir, Gothic, Hemingway, Shakespearean, Poetic, Minimalist, and more.
  • Prompt library — curated starter prompts per genre, one click to load.
  • Drafts — save, reload, and delete drafts stored only on your device.
  • Import / export — drop a .md / .txt to load; export as Markdown; live preview.

Tech

  • Client-only Astro static site with React islands.
  • AI via g4f (gpt4free) through @chirag127/oz-ai, with multi-provider failover — no API key.
  • PWA-installable.
  • 100% in-browser: no upload, no signup, no server. Drafts stay in IndexedDB on your device.
